OPEN KNOWLEDGE, WITH ROOM TO READ

A clearer view of a Wikibook.

Read comfortably. Find the authors. Understand how to cite and reuse what you discover.

Open a book or chapter

Opening a page contacts English Wikibooks and sends the requested title or revision. No login credentials or editor files are sent. Reading content stays in memory until you leave. This release supports English Wikibooks; other Wikimedia projects open on their own sites.

Paste a link above or try the education book.
